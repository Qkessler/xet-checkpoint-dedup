## Verdict rule

Fixed before measuring ([ADR 0001](adr/0001-test-the-implied-claim-with-preset-thresholds.md)): transferred bytes within 3x of the sparse delta **holds**, at least 50% of the anchor **does not hold**, anything between **depends**. The sparse delta is changed elements x 6 bytes (a 4-byte index plus a 2-byte bf16 value).
